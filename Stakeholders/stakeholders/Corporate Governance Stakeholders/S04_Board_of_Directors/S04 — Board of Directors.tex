\section*{Description}

The Board of Directors is a corporate governing body responsible for overseeing the company's strategic direction, management and corporate governance. In the context of GOSPL, the Board formulates proposals for shareholder consideration, provides relevant supporting information and reviews shareholder voting outcomes.

The Board is concerned primarily with the substance and integrity of its proposals rather than the operational execution of voting. Internal Board deliberations, strategic decision-making and proposal approval processes remain outside GOSPL. Proposals may also originate from other entitled parties.
