# S04 – Board of Directors

---

## Description

The Board of Directors is a corporate governing body responsible for overseeing the company's strategic direction, management and corporate governance. In the context of GOSPL, the Board formulates proposals for shareholder consideration, provides relevant supporting information and reviews shareholder voting outcomes.

The Board is concerned primarily with the substance and integrity of its proposals rather than the operational execution of voting. Internal Board deliberations, strategic decision-making and proposal approval processes remain outside GOSPL. Proposals may also originate from other entitled parties.

## Stakeholder Concerns

### Distinct Concerns

| ID | Concern | Stakeholder Expectation |
|---|---|---|
| S04-C01 | Proposal Management | We want to submit matters proposed for shareholder consideration, together with their relevant supporting documentation. |
| S04-C02 | Proposal Integrity | We want the intended meaning and content of our proposals to be accurately preserved throughout the shareholder voting process, without unauthorized modifications. |

## Extra Information

### Conceptual Proposal Lifecycle

| Stage | Proposed Responsible Stakeholder |
|---|---|
| Prepared Proposal | Board of Directors (outside GOSPL) or another entitled proposer |
| Submitted | Authorized Board representative or other authorized proposer |
| Under Review | Relevant governance stakeholders |
| Published | AGM Administrators |
| Voting Open | AGM Administrators; shareholders and proxy representatives exercise voting rights |
| Voting Closed | AGM Administrators |
| Results Under Verification | Authorized verification stakeholders |
| Results Published | Authorized publication of voting outcomes |

This is an illustrative lifecycle, not a finalized state model or mandatory approval workflow. The Board may track proposal progress without having permission to perform every operation.

### Candidate Profile and Permissions

| Information or Operation | Proposed Access |
|---|---|
| Board profile | View |
| Associated Board members or representatives | View |
| Submit prepared proposals | Permitted |
| Submit supporting documentation | Permitted |

### Scope Boundaries

| Candidate Scope Within GOSPL | Outside GOSPL |
|---|---|
| Submission of prepared proposals | Internal Board meetings |
| Supporting documentation management | Corporate strategy formulation |
| Proposal status tracking | Internal Board deliberations |
| Authorized proposal amendments | Internal proposal approval processes |
| Access to voting outcomes | Implementation of adopted resolutions |

### Relationship with Other Governance Stakeholders

| Stakeholder | Proposed Responsibility |
|---|---|
| S04 – Board of Directors | Substance of its proposals |
| S05 – AGM Administrators | Operational execution of shareholder voting |
| S06 – Legal Counsel & Compliance Officers | Legal and procedural review |
| S07 – Auditors & Scrutineers | Verification of voting activities and results |

These responsibilities do not necessarily establish a hierarchy within GOSPL.

### Validation Notes

- The Board's direct participation, profile, permissions, proposal lifecycle and prioritization are working assumptions requiring validation against Kesko's actual AGM procedures and applicable rules.
- Legal review, verification and formal result declaration are distinct responsibilities; mandatory approval stages and responsible officials have not been established.
- GOSPL must accommodate proposals originating from entitled parties other than the Board.
- The concerns and conflicts are preliminary stakeholder analysis findings, not statements validated with actual Kesko stakeholders.
