# GOSPL – Stakeholder Register

**Reference company:** Kesko Oyj  
**Purpose:** Task 2 – Identify and Analyze Stakeholders  
**Status:** Working draft – initial classification

GOSPL is a shareholder voting platform based on Kesko's corporate governance context. This register records the proposed stakeholder groups. Individual concerns, conflicts, importance and influence will be developed iteratively. Assumed concerns will be distinguished from validated information. Laws, regulations and other non-stakeholder requirements sources will be documented separately under Task 3.

## Individual stakeholder analysis

For each stakeholder, record:

- Description and role
- Main concerns and expected outcomes
- Potential conflicts with other stakeholders
- Importance and rationale
- Influence and rationale
- Evidence/source and validation status

Formal requirements and architectural decisions are outside the scope of this initial register.
