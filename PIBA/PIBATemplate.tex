\pibapart{Problem}
% What is wrong, missing, or hard today, and for whom?
% Describe the situation today, not your solution. Name the system, the users,
% and the concrete pain. Quantify where possible.
Traditional shareholder voting processes restrict effective participation in Annual General Meetings (AGMs) due to rigid deadlines, complex procedures, and varying governance rules. The following problems affect individual and institutional shareholders:

\begin{itemize}
  \item Investors must submit voting instructions before shareholder meetings due to early deadlines, which their ability to revise decisions when new information emerges.
  \item The lack of real-time power-of-attorney capabilities prevents shareholders from delegating their voting rights to legal counsel before or during the meeting.
  \item Differences in share classes and voting rights complicate the accurate allocation of voting power, potentially leading to misrepresentation of shareholders' influence.
  \item Certain shareholders can block a resolution with a veto vote. The system must let only eligible holders use the veto within the voting window and apply it correctly in the tally.
\end{itemize}

% ---------------------------------------------------------------------------
\pibapart{Idea}
% What is your proposed solution, and what does its architecture look like?
% The architecture is the heart of your idea.
We believe that a solution to the above problems is a Shareholder Governance Platform with the following functional and non-functional features:

\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Identity \& Delegation Gateway:} Authenticate users and facilitate live proxy switching to instantly assign or revoke Power of Attorney to legal counsel.
  \item \textbf{Dynamic Rules \& Weighting Engine:} Calculate voting power using share classes and ownership records, while validating and applying veto effects to outcomes.
  \item \textbf{Live Ballot Broker:} Accept and update voting instructions within deadlines, allowing investors to revise decisions and receive real-time updates.
  \item \textbf{Security \& Auditability:} Ensure that all votes are logged in an immutable audit trail for legal compliance.
\end{itemize}

% ---------------------------------------------------------------------------
\pibapart{Benefits}
% What improves if the idea works, and how would you show it?
% Link each benefit back to the problem.
\begin{itemize}
  \item Flexible Voting -- Enables shareholders to revise voting decisions as new information emerges, within applicable voting deadlines.
  \item Real-Time Proxy Delegation -- Enables shareholders to assign or revoke voting authority to authorized representatives in real time, where permitted.
  \item Accurate Voting Power -- Ensures correct allocation of voting weights based on share ownership, share classes, and applicable voting rights.
  \item Enforced Veto Rights -- Prevents unauthorized veto usage through eligibility validation and ensures correct application of veto rights in voting outcomes.
\end{itemize}

% ---------------------------------------------------------------------------
\pibapart{Action}
% What will you build and evaluate, in which steps, within 3 months?
% Mark the minimum deliverable and keep some buffer.
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Sprint 1} -- Research shareholder meetings and voting procedures, identify functional and non-functional requirements, and develop a domain model covering key stakeholders and voting rules.
  \item \textbf{Sprint 2} -- Design the system architecture, defining component responsibilities, API contracts, security boundaries, and event-driven interactions for voting and proxy delegation.
  \item \textbf{Sprint 3} -- Develop a prototype integrating the Live Ballot Broker, Delegation Gateway, and Dynamic Rules Engine using a mocked share registry to demonstrate vote revisions, proxy delegation, weighted voting, and veto enforcement.
\end{itemize}
Team split: 2 Requirements Analysts, 2 Technical Developers, 1 Version Management Coordinator, 1 Domain Modelling Specialist.
